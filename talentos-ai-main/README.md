TalentOS — AI Career Intelligence Platform

Transforming resumes into careers with AI-powered analysis, optimization, and recruiter simulation.

🚀 Overview

TalentOS is a modern AI-powered Career Intelligence Operating System designed to help students, job seekers, and professionals improve their employability through deep career analytics.

This platform goes beyond a normal Resume Analyzer by integrating:

Resume Intelligence
ATS Optimization
Skill Gap Analysis
Hiring Probability Prediction
Salary Trends
Market Trends
LinkedIn/GitHub/LeetCode Analysis
Recruiter Simulation
Portfolio Scoring
Project Strength Evaluation
AI Career Roadmaps
🎯 Core Mission

Build a startup-grade AI Career OS that acts like:

“Your Personal AI Career Strategist”

TalentOS helps users answer:

Why is my resume getting rejected?
What skills am I missing?
What are my chances of getting hired?
What salary can I expect?
How strong is my GitHub / LinkedIn / Portfolio?
What projects should I build?
Which courses should I take?
